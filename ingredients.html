BM

Bacopa Monnieri

The memory herb

Bacopa Monnieri (also called brahmi) is a creeping herb that has been used in Ayurvedic wellness traditions for more than a thousand years, traditionally to support memory, learning, and calm, clear thinking.

  • Traditionally used to support memory formation and recall
  • Associated with the active compounds known as bacosides
  • A classic 'adaptogen' valued for steady, non-stimulant support
  • One of the most-studied botanicals in the nootropic world

Why it's in NeuroVitol: Bacopa is the cornerstone of NeuroVitol's memory support. It's the ingredient people most associate with 'remembering names, lists, and next steps' — which is exactly the everyday cognitive job NeuroVitol is built to support.

LM

Lion's Mane

The focus mushroom

Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus) is a distinctive, shaggy functional mushroom that became one of the most talked-about nootropic ingredients of 2025–2026 for its association with focus, clarity, and neurological wellness.

  • Popular for supporting focus and mental clarity
  • Associated with healthy neurological wellness
  • A favorite 'modern nootropic' ingredient in 2026
  • Non-stimulant — supports clarity without the jitters

Why it's in NeuroVitol: Lion's Mane brings the 'clear-headed focus' dimension to NeuroVitol. Where Bacopa anchors memory, Lion's Mane is associated with the sharp, switched-on feeling of being able to concentrate and follow through.

GB

Ginkgo Biloba

The circulation botanical

Ginkgo Biloba is one of the oldest tree species on earth and one of the most widely used botanicals in the world, traditionally taken to support healthy circulation and overall brain performance.

  • Supports healthy blood circulation
  • Traditionally used for overall brain performance
  • One of the most widely used botanicals globally
  • Complements the formula's focus and memory ingredients

Why it's in NeuroVitol: The brain is an oxygen- and blood-flow-hungry organ. Ginkgo is included to support the healthy circulation that underpins steady mental performance throughout the day.

PS

Phosphatidylserine

The brain-cell phospholipid

Phosphatidylserine (PS) is a phospholipid — a type of fat — that is naturally concentrated in brain-cell membranes, where it supports healthy cell structure and the signaling behind focus and memory.

  • Supports healthy brain-cell membrane structure
  • Involved in the cognitive activity behind focus & memory
  • A well-known ingredient in premium brain formulas
  • Works structurally rather than as a stimulant

Why it's in NeuroVitol: PS is the 'structural' member of the blend. Bacopa, Lion's Mane and Ginkgo support how your brain performs; Phosphatidylserine supports the healthy cell structure that performance is built on.

B12

Vitamin B12

The neuro-nutrient

Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) is an essential vitamin your body can't make on its own. It supports normal neurological function and healthy energy metabolism — a foundational nutrient for steady mental stamina.

  • Supports normal neurological function
  • Supports healthy energy metabolism
  • An essential vitamin many adults run low on
  • Underpins steady, all-day mental stamina

Why it's in NeuroVitol: B12 is the foundation. Even the best brain-support botanicals work best when the underlying neurological and energy systems are nourished — which is why NeuroVitol rounds out the formula with B12.
