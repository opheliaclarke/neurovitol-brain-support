Bacopa, Lion's Mane & Ginkgo: The Brain-Support Ingredient Guide

NeuroVitol uses five brain-support ingredients — and unlike many 2026 formulas, you can actually read and pronounce all of them. Here's a plain-English guide to what each one is, what it's traditionally used for, and why it earns a place in the formula.

Bacopa Monnieri

Also called brahmi, Bacopa is a creeping herb used in Ayurvedic wellness traditions for over a thousand years, traditionally to support memory, learning, and calm, clear thinking. Its active compounds are known as bacosides. It's the cornerstone of NeuroVitol's memory support — the ingredient most associated with “remembering names, lists, and next steps.”

Lion's Mane

A distinctive, shaggy functional mushroom (Hericium erinaceus) that became one of the most talked-about nootropic ingredients of 2026. It's popular for supporting focus, mental clarity, and neurological wellness — the “switched-on, clear-headed” feeling — without stimulant jitters.

Ginkgo Biloba

From one of the oldest tree species on earth, Ginkgo is among the most widely used botanicals in the world. It's traditionally taken to support healthy circulation and overall brain performance — important because the brain is so dependent on healthy blood flow.

Phosphatidylserine (PS)

A phospholipid — a type of fat — naturally concentrated in brain-cell membranes, where it supports healthy cell structure and the signaling behind focus and memory. It's the “structural” member of the blend, supporting the foundation that performance is built on.

Vitamin B12

An essential vitamin your body can't make on its own. It supports normal neurological function and healthy energy metabolism — a foundational nutrient for steady mental stamina that many adults run low on.

How they work together

Four jobs plus a foundation: Bacopa anchors memory, Lion's Mane brings focus, Ginkgo supports circulation, Phosphatidylserine supports cell structure, and B12 nourishes the underlying systems. Five ingredients, one simple daily capsule.
